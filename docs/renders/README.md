## Bring-up probe drawings

`probe-*.svg` are a different thing and are generated by a different script —
`hardware/scripts/probe_points.py`, not `render.sh`. They are line drawings of
the **solder-mask openings** (the metal a probe tip can actually touch) with
every probe point in `hardware/fab/test/PROBE-POINTS.json` badged and keyed.

| File | Region |
|---|---|
| `probe-overview.svg` | whole board, all 38 points |
| `probe-power.svg` | USB-C, CH224K PD, AP63200 buck |
| `probe-mcu.svg` | ESP32-S3, LEDs, switches, debug header |
| `probe-ethernet.svg` | W5500, crystal, SPI, magnetics |
| `probe-can.svg` | ADM3053, termination, CAN jacks, isolated domain shaded |

The script asserts every probe's designator, pad and net against
`canary.kicad_pcb` and refuses to run if one has moved, so a badge cannot
quietly drift off its pad. Use them with
[`BENCH-BRINGUP.md`](../../hardware/fab/test/BENCH-BRINGUP.md).

## What the renders show

- The **isolation barrier** is visible as a band across the middle of the board.
  U201 (ADM3053) straddles it; everything below and left is the isolated CAN
  domain (CAN_GND / VISO), everything above is system ground.
- **U301's antenna overhangs the top board edge**, as intended — the module's
  all-layer keepout falls off the board rather than eating copper.
- The three **etherCON jacks** along the bottom edge, and the USB-C at top right.

## What the renders CANNOT show

**No 3D models exist for the four most mechanically critical parts.** They
render as bare pads inside a white outline:

| Part | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| J201 / J202 / J401 | Neutrik etherCON NE8FBH. Footprint drawn from the vendor DXF; the courtyard is ~14.4 mm but the real front plate is 25 mm |
| J102 | USB-C receptacle (TYPE-C-31-M-12) |
| T401 | PT61017PEL Ethernet magnetics |

So **the renders do not prove the connectors fit or that their mating faces
reach the board edge.** Two measurements that need checking against the real
parts' mechanical drawings:

- **J102** body face sits at y = 19.68 with the board edge at y = 18.95 — the
  receptacle is recessed **0.73 mm** behind the edge. A USB-C plug's nose is
  tapered so this is probably fine, but it should be flush or overhanging.
- **etherCON courtyards** end at y = 97.77 with the board edge at y = 100.05 —
  **2.28 mm** of board past the courtyard. Whether that is correct depends on
  where the NE8FBH's mating face sits relative to its pads, which is not
  captured in the footprint.

## Silkscreen

The renders make the 36 silkscreen DRC warnings concrete and legible-looking
problems rather than abstract counts. Worst cases:

- `D304D303D302D301` / `R305R304R303R302` — the left-hand LED and resistor
  columns collapse into two unreadable blobs
- `R40640R408409` — the four MDI termination resistors
- `C204C205 C206`, `C401C408C409`, `C301C302C303` — overlapping

Gerbers are plotted with `--subtract-soldermask`, so silk is clipped off pads
and this will not affect solderability. It does affect anyone trying to identify
a part during rework. Scheduled for rev B.
