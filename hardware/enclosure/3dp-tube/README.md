# CANary — one-piece tube enclosure

Output of `hardware/enclosure/gen_case_3dp.py --tube`: the case body as one part that
prints standing on its rear end, plus two flush end plates screwed to it (8 × M3 into
heat-set inserts), the LED window insert and the switch cover. Documented in
[`../3dp/README.md`](../3dp/README.md); sliced as `../canary-case-absgf-0.4-tube.3mf`
(`bambu/build.sh absgf-0.4-tube`).
